One connected path, from a follower to a returning customer.

Getting attention isn't the same as getting customers.

Most small businesses already have followers, visitors, questions and leads. What's usually missing is the connective tissue between them — so a lot of that attention just leaks away.

Without a system

  1. Someone follows your page
  2. They ask a question in the comments
  3. No one answers for a day or two
  4. They find someone else instead

With a system

  1. Someone follows your page
  2. They visit your website
  3. Your AI assistant answers instantly
  4. The lead is captured automatically
  5. They're guided to WhatsApp
  6. They book, buy, or ask for a quote
  7. Automation follows up, no one forgotten
